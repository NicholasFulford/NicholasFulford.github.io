This project applies persistent homology to characterise regime change in financial markets. It began as an extension of a previous paper by <a href='https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3394704'>Basu and Li (2019)</a>, which used topological data analysis as an input to a machine-learning early-warning system for market downturns. Here, persistent homology is used more directly: persistence entropy computed on Takens-embedded return series is tracked as a standalone turbulence signal, capturing local phase-space deformations that could indicate market turbulence. We have been working with a digital investment platform who were interested in our preliminary findings and have shared additional market data with us to expand the investigation.

This project is being carried out in parallel with 'Topological Signatures Of Adversarial Influence In LLMs'.
