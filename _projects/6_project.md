This project is being carried out in parallel with 'Topological Signatures Of Adversarial Influence In LLMs', with both projects applying the ideas of persistent homology and topological data analysis. 

This project began as an extension of a previous paper by <a href='https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3394704'>Basu and Li (2019)</a>, shifting the investigation from traditional econometric time-series analysis to using persistent homology to capture local phase-space deformations that could indicate turbulence in financial markets.
